## Placeholder rules

- Keep placeholders exactly as in English: `{{name}}`, `{count}`, `{plural}`, `{title}`, `{days}`, `{date}`, `{time}`, `{lang}`, `{size}`, `{error}`, `{name}`, `{num}`, `{type}`, `{due}`, `{total}`, `{active}`, `{contracts}`, `{links}`, `{notes}`, `{cards}`, `{connections}`, `{rows}`, `{fields}`, `{all}`, `{all}`, `{query}`.
- Never translate placeholder names.
- Right-to-left: keep punctuation natural (Urdu uses ؟ and ۔), do not mirror numbers.
